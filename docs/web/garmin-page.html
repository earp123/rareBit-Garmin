How the signal reaches your wrist

Garmin watches can't listen to PRO Flags directly. A rareBit Relay bridges the gap — it hears every linked Flag and forwards each signal to the watch over Bluetooth LE.

Every flag, its own buzz

Distinct vibration patterns per Flag mean you know which AR is signaling without looking down.
