On the wrist

Captured on a Venu 3. Countdown front and centre, count-up below, progress ring round the bezel. A flag press flashes the flag and its number.

How the signal reaches your wrist

Garmin watches can't listen to PRO Flags directly. A rareBit Relay bridges the gap — it hears every linked Flag and forwards each signal to the watch over Bluetooth LE.

Every flag, its own buzz

Distinct vibration patterns per Flag mean you know which AR is signaling without looking down.

Installing the .prg
  1. Unzip the download — inside is a single rareBitGarmin-<model>.prg.
  2. Plug the watch into a computer over USB and copy the .prg into the watch's GARMIN/APPS folder. On a Mac, if the watch doesn't show up as a drive, use an MTP app such as OpenMTP.
  3. Unplug. rareBit appears in the watch's app list.
