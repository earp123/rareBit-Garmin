Installing

  1. Unzip the download — inside is a single rareBitGarmin-<model>.prg.
  2. Plug the watch into a computer over USB and copy the .prg into GARMIN/APPS. On a Mac, if the watch doesn't show up as a drive, use an MTP app such as OpenMTP.
  3. Unplug. rareBit appears in the watch's app list.

Using it

  1. Open rareBit on the watch. It finds your Relay on its own — no pairing screen.
  2. SELECT starts and pauses the match. BACK opens settings: interval, half, stoppage timer.
  3. No Relay in range? After 15 s it runs as a plain match timer.
